Quran Word Catcherكَلِمَاتُ ٱلْقُرْآن

Rebuild each verse word by word. Pinch your thumb and index finger to catch a word, then place it in the next empty frame at the bottom. Verses are built right to left. When you catch a word, you hear it recited and see its meaning in English and Persian. When you finish a verse, it is recited in full and appears whole with its English, Persian and Pashto translations.

Requires an internet connection. Text and translations load from quran.com. Camera mode works best in Chrome. Allow camera access when asked. Your video stays on your device.
